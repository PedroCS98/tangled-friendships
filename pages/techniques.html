Our Techniques

At Tangled Friendships, we work with three handmade techniques:

  • Macramé bracelets — knotted by hand into unique, customisable pieces.
  • Kumihimo cords — traditional Japanese braids, elegant and versatile.
  • Obijime ties — decorative cords worn around the waist, inspired by the kimono tradition.

The Art of Macramé

Macramé is a millenary handmade technique that transforms simple strings and knots into art full of meaning. Each knot represents connection, union and strength. It is a reflection of the connections we build in life — intertwined, strong and unique.

Macramé is more than a decoration: it's time, dedication and care. Each piece is handmade, loaded with craftsmanship and human value. With Macramé, each creation is different. There are infinite possibilities in styles, colours and customisations. It's an art as unique as each person.

Kumihimo

Kumihimo is a traditional Japanese artwork and craftwork for making braids and cords. The name literally means "gathered threads". Kumihimo braids can be used as simple and elegant hair accessories, just like a hair tie. Kumihimo cords are also used as obijime — cords that tie around the obi of a kimono. In the past, kumihimo cords were used in samurai armour too.

Obijime

Obijime are decorative cords traditionally worn with kimonos, tied around the obi to keep it in place and add an elegant finishing touch. At Tangled Friendships, we craft obijime using the kumihimo braiding technique, honouring the Japanese tradition with handmade quality.
