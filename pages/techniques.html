Our Techniques

Kumihimo

Kumihimo is a traditional Japanese artwork and craftwork for making braids and cords. The name literally means "gathered threads". Kumihimo braids can be used as simple and elegant hair accessories, just like a hair tie. Kumihimo cords are also used as obijime — cords that tie around the obi of a kimono. In the past, kumihimo cords were used in samurai armour too.

Obijime

Obijime are decorative cords traditionally worn with kimonos, tied around the obi to keep it in place and add an elegant finishing touch. At Tangled Friendships, we craft obijime using the kumihimo braiding technique, honouring the Japanese tradition with handmade quality.

For more information about the Kumihimo braiding technique watch this video from the YouTube channel ROANOKE.
