Two Friends, One Thread

Tangled Friendships began the way most good things do — with two people, a shared table, and far too many spools of thread.

How It Started

Vera and Ana met in their first year of college, back in 2021. Five years and countless conversations later, what started as a friendship became something you can hold in your hands.

It began with bracelets. One evening, one technique, one piece made for each other — the kind of gift that means more than what it cost. Friends asked for their own. Then friends of friends. Six months ago, they decided to stop calling it a hobby.

Tangled Friendships is what happened next. Every piece is still made by the two of them, by hand, at the same table where it all started.

Every knot we tie is a small act of patience. There are no machines here — only time, thread, and two pairs of hands.

What We Believe

Our Mission

To make pieces that carry meaning — handmade, one at a time, for people who value the difference between something bought and something made.

Our Vision

To grow slowly and deliberately, exploring every craft that begins with a thread, without ever losing the hands behind the work.

Our Values

  • Made once — no two pieces are ever quite the same.
  • Made slowly — good things take the time they take.
  • Made together — every piece passes through both our hands.

The Two of Us

Vera

The fox. Curious, quick to try a new technique, and the one who says yes to the complicated commissions.

Ana

The panda. Patient, precise, and the reason every knot sits exactly where it should.
