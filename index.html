The Art Behind Every Piece

At Tangled Friendships, every piece starts with a thread and a technique passed down through generations. Our bracelets are crafted using macramé — an ancient knotting art that transforms simple cords into intricate, wearable pieces full of character and meaning.

Beyond bracelets, we also work with two traditional Japanese crafts — kumihimo, the art of braided cords, and obijime, the decorative ties worn with kimonos — bringing their elegance into everyday wear.

Each piece is made entirely by hand. No machines, no shortcuts. Just patience, skill and care woven into every knot.

Caring for Your Piece

  1. Keep it dry — remove before showering, swimming or washing up.
  2. Avoid perfumes and lotions directly on the cords.
  3. Store it flat, away from direct sunlight to protect the colours.
  4. Gently reshape the knots with your fingers if they loosen.
  5. Worn regularly, the cords soften and mould to your wrist over time.
